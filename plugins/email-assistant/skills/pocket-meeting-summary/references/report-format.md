# Executive meeting report format

Start with a title and compact meeting date/title/attendees/recording ID-link metadata, disclosing source limitations. Use these unnumbered headings in this exact order, omitting sections without genuine content. Do not fill absent sections with empty tables or claims that nothing happened.

| Heading | Required treatment |
| --- | --- |
| Needs your attention | Explicit approvals, unresolved decisions, urgent blockers, material risks and time-sensitive/overdue commitments; supported consequence, intervention and timing. Distinguish meeting requests from analytical implications. |
| Executive summary | Stand-alone purpose, substantive outcomes, implications and unresolved matters, prioritized by significance with context for an absent reader. |
| Decisions made | `Decision \| Owner \| Rationale` table. Only settled choices, approvals, rejections or agreements; proposals/preferences are not decisions. Unknown owner is `Unassigned`; unstated rationale is `Not stated`. Speaker is not automatically owner. |
| Action items | `Task \| Assignee \| Deadline` table. Concrete tasks/obligations, distinct from decisions. Missing assignee OR deadline is `Unassigned`. Attribute self-commitments only to identified speakers; do not assign by role or expertise. Preserve relative timing; add an unambiguous date only alongside the original wording, otherwise flag uncertainty. |
| Risks, blockers and open questions | Distinguish potential harms, current obstacles and unanswered questions with stated effects/dependencies; label analytical interpretations. |
| Commitments made to external parties | Actual external promises with recipient, outcome, owner/timing if stated; missing owner/deadline is `Unassigned`. Distinguish aspirations; cross-reference related tasks rather than inventing a second obligation. |
| Key numbers, quoted with context | Exact numerical wording, units/currency, period, known speaker and stated actual/estimate/target/projection status. Keep qualifiers/ranges and conflicting numbers. Never present derived totals as quoted figures. |
| Sentiment and tone signals | Only transcript-supported words/interactions, with examples/timestamps. Separate observation and tentative interpretation; no hidden motives, psychology or unavailable vocal tone. Omit weak evidence. |
| Full narrative summary | Detailed topic-organized context, arguments, alternatives, outcomes, unresolved threads and meaningful dissent. No transcript dump or invented connective facts. |
| Notable quotes | Consequential verbatim excerpts with context, supplied speaker labels and timestamps when available. Never silently repair or reconstruct quotes. |
